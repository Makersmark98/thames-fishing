How surf ratings work · sources

Each break gets a score from the swell's size, period and direction compared with the way the break faces, plus the wind (offshore is good, onshore is bad). Breaks that need more swell (Whangamatā Bar, Kuaotunu point) lose points when it's small. Swell: surf-forecast.com (issued Fri 27 Nov, 6pm). Break details: Waikato Regional Council surf break report (based on the Wavetrack guide) and surf-forecast.com break guides. "Stoke" is the guide's quality rating out of 10. Tides are for the east coast (Hot Water Beach). These are estimates: always check the surf yourself before you paddle out.

Sources and caveats

The zones are drawn from the coordinates in the Hauraki Gulf Marine Protection Bill. The final Act uses survey plans, so check exact boundaries in the MarineMate app or on DOC's maps before you dive. Rules: DOC (HPAs), MPI (inner Gulf cray closure), MPI (CRA2 limit, telson clip), MPI (sizes, berried, soft shell), Boating NZ (2026 packhorse limit), MPI (caulerpa). Dive spots come from NZ tourism, AA (Gemstone Bay) and local spearos. Rules change, so always check the NZ Fishing Rules app.

This week by the moon

Bite times are when the moon is overhead or underfoot. At Thames they usually fall near the low-tide turn, because the moon drives the tide. Night names follow the maramataka in Te Ara, counted from the 9 Nov new moon. That list comes from Ngāti Kahungunu, and Hauraki iwi may name and rate the nights differently.

How ratings work · sources

Wind and chop weigh most, then tide timing in daylight, then pressure and moon. These are my estimates, not a guarantee. Chop heights are estimated from the wind.
